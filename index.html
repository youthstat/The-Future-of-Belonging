About the fieldwork: Between May and August 2026, our Danish lead organisation, Together is Better, coordinated data collection across all seven partner countries — running the online questionnaire, organising local focus groups, and compiling interviews with youth workers. That first round of data collection is now closed. The findings below reflect what 840 young people told us. A new, open questionnaire for our next research phase is live further down this page - we'd love your answers too.

What we did with this

From numbers to programme design

65% fear discussing mental health

Psychological safety comes first

Daily reflection circles, quiet rooms, and mindfulness sessions led by altera lab are built into the exchange programme.

72% say online bonds feel shallow

Digital wellbeing is not optional

A dedicated Digital Wellbeing Lab, run by Together is Better, teaches critical thinking and healthier time online.

78% are ready to act, but don't know how

Local initiatives carry the impact home

Each national team designs and runs its own "Connection Café" or workshop in their community after the exchange.

Ukraine (71%) & Armenia (67%) highest

Priority for the most affected

Over 50% of programme places are reserved for participants with fewer opportunities — displaced youth, rural participants, and NEETs.
